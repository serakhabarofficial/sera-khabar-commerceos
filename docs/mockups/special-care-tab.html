Special Care: a place for orders and customers that need a human

Steadfast only tells us where a parcel is. It never tells us that the rider called and said the customer refused it, or that a VIP wants delivery after 6 pm. This tab holds what the team learns on the phone, and keeps it in front of them until someone closes it.

What the merchant asked (voice notes, 22 Oct)
  • Gather special customers in one place“এ জাতীয় customer গুলা ঐ জায়গায় জড়ো করতে পারি”
  • VIPs, heavy parcels, standing requirementsRepeat customers who always leave instructions about their parcel.
  • Mark them urgent and serve them separately“urgent যাতে আমরা mark করতে পারি”
The calls you described
  • The rider calls“কাস্টমার নিচ্ছে না”The order is already with the courier. Steadfast still says In Transit.
  • The customer calls“অর্ডারটা কাল পাঠাবেন না, ৩ দিন পর পাঠান”The order hasn't shipped yet.
What already exists
  • On Hold stops an orderIt has 10 reasons, including “পরে পার্সেল নিতে চান” with a return date. The order goes back to Pending on that date.
  • Hold can't help after dispatchOnce the parcel is with Steadfast, there's nowhere to record what the rider or the customer said.
  • Nothing remembers a customerEach order starts blank, even for a VIP's 14th order.

The model: two layers
Layer 1 · Order flag

A case on one order

Who called (rider, customer or team), a reason, a note with what was said, an optional follow-up date, and an urgent switch. Every later call adds a line to its log. It ends when someone resolves it with an outcome: delivered after the call, rescheduled, returned or cancelled.

Layer 2 · Customer tag

Something true about a person

VIP, heavy parcel, regular customer, or a standing instruction. It's attached to the phone number, so the next order from that number opens a flag in Special Care automatically, with the instruction already filled in.

No webhookOnly people add flags or resolve them. Courier sync never touches them.
Status stays the sameA flag sits beside the order status. An In Transit order stays In Transit.
One open case per orderNew calls add to the existing case so the history stays in one place.
Visible everywhereA small dot on the row in every other tab, and a banner in the order editor.

Questions for the merchant before we plan the build

Six things to decide

What does “urgent” change?Does it only sort to the top, or should it also block bulk Send to Courier until someone opens the order?
What is an “OAT” parcel exactly?Is it a weight threshold (for example, over 5 kg), or a specific product? If it's a threshold, we can tag these orders automatically.
Should customer tags flag every new order?Or only show a badge on the order without adding it to this tab?
Who resolves a case?Anyone on the team, the person it's assigned to, or only an admin?
Should standing instructions print on the label?For example, “সন্ধ্যা ৬টার পরে ডেলিভারি” as the courier note sent to Steadfast.
How long do resolved cases stay visible?For example, a Resolved filter that keeps the last 7 days.
